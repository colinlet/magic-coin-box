# 🛠️ MagicCoinBox - Implementation Guide

This guide details how to implement the *MagicCoinBox* system at home. Both parents must read it carefully and reach a consensus before starting.

---

## 1. Hardware & Environment Preparation

Before starting, you need to prepare the following items:

1. **Transparent Bank**: A transparent plastic or glass jar for storing coins. The transparent material provides intuitive visual feedback of "wealth growth".
2. **Physical Coins**: It is recommended to buy game coins or colorful chips with some weight.
3. **Visual Chart**: Open `rewards_system_en.html`, select the age stage corresponding to your child, click the "Print Current Chart" button in the upper right corner, preferably with physical photos or illustrations, and post it in a conspicuous place where the child can easily see it (such as the refrigerator door or the wall of the children's room).

---

## 2. The "Four Iron Rules" for Parents

These are the cornerstones to prevent the system from collapsing. Please strictly abide by them:

1. **Immediate Issuance, Reinforce the Process**: Give the coin immediately after the behavior occurs, and explicitly praise the specific behavior verbally (e.g., "You didn't lose your temper today and communicated well, this is your reward"). Never delay until night or the next day.
2. **Absolutely No Fines (Only Rewards, No Deductions)**: Poor performance only means "missing the opportunity to earn". **You must never confiscate coins already deposited in the jar.** You must protect the safety of the child's personal assets and maintain the system's credit baseline.
3. **Stick to Principles, No Credit**: Even if short by 1 coin, they cannot redeem a higher-level reward. Everyone is equal before the rules. This is the key to delayed gratification training.
4. **Clear Challenge Standards, Prevent "Farming"**: For example, reciting a poem must be "newly learned and fluent", and exercising must "reach the specified duration/intensity". Prevent children from obtaining coins through invalid repetition.

## 3. Core Mechanism Analysis

### 1. Dual-Track Income
*   **Base Salary**: Earned by completing daily self-care and emotional management (e.g., "Self-Care Little Expert"), establishing a sense of security and habit foundation.
*   **Performance Bonus**: Earned by completing challenging tasks that consume physical or mental energy (e.g., learning a new skill), encouraging self-breakthrough.

### 2. Innovative Highlight: "3 out of 5" Fault Tolerance Mechanism
To address the "broken windows theory" easily triggered by daily chores, a "3 out of 5" passing grade is set. For daily tasks, completing any 3 out of 5 items earns the reward. This protects enthusiasm while allowing for minor flaws in life.

---

## 4. Semester Transition & Exit Mechanism (Dynamic Iteration)

To solve the problems of habit internalization and inflation, the system must iterate in units of "semesters" (or fixed periods, such as every 3 months).

### 1. End-of-Term Clearance (1 Week Before Break)
*   **Ritual**: Hold an "End-of-Term Shopping Festival".
*   **Rule**: Announce the end of this cycle. All coins must be spent to redeem big prizes. **Ensure old coins are not reset to zero but perfectly consumed, and absolutely do not flow into the new semester.**

### 2. New Semester Kick-off (3 Days Before School)
*   **Habit Free-of-Charge (Delisting Mechanism)**: Remove habits fully mastered last semester (like dressing independently) from the earning list. Affirm their growth: "You are a big kid now and do this very well even without coins."
*   **Issue New Tasks (Difficulty Upgrade)**: Introduce higher-difficulty tasks suitable for the new age group.
*   **New Rewards**: Add more experience-based and decision-making privilege rewards, gradually replacing material rewards.
